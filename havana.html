Havana Caravan Bar

Deliverables
Editable social templates, digital About Us document

Havana Caravan Bar is a mobile bar and bartender hire business serving Geelong, Melbourne and regional Victoria. Owners Emma and Jacob requested a polished About Us document to send to new enquiries.

They were looking for a way to keep their social feed consistent without hiring a designer for every post. I built a set of editable Canva templates they can update and reuse.
