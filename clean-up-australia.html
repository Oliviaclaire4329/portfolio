Clean Up Australia

Role
Volunteer designer
Deliverables
31-page publication, designed for screen and print

I was selected as the designer for Clean Up Australia’s 2022 Rubbish Report, a 31-page publication turning a year of clean-up data into infographics and data visualisation. With full creative freedom and three weeks to deliver, I set it up in InDesign so it was ready for both digital release and print.

Previous reports all followed a similar pattern of greens and tree motifs. With no set rules, I wanted to bring something totally new, so I introduced a broader palette and gave each type of rubbish its own colour, such as plastics, paper and cardboard, and glass, so readers could recognise each material at a glance.

The report needed minimal revisions, was featured on Clean Up Australia’s website, and the team invited me back to design their Review of Operations on a similar timeline. Here is a selection of my favourite spreads.

On a very tight schedule Liv was professional, flexible and co-operative. She was a pleasure to work with, interpreting the brief perfectly and the combination of her creative input and eye for detail meant the end product was exactly what we were after.

Nicki MartinMarketing and Communications Manager, Clean Up Australia
