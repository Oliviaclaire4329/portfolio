Clean Up Australia

Deliverables
31-page digital publication design

I was selected to design Clean Up Australia’s 2022 Rubbish Report, and the team gave me full creative freedom to present the year’s impact through infographics and data visualisation.

While researching, I noticed past reports mostly leaned on green to signal nature. I introduced a broader palette and gave each type of rubbish its own colour, such as plastics, paper and cardboard, and glass, so readers could recognise each material at a glance across all pages.
