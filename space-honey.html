Space Honey

Deliverables
Single artwork ×2, social assets

Described by Happy Mag as “a psych-rock conglomerate swimming in all kinds of good ’60s flavours”, Space Honey is a Geelong artist following up their 2025 EP Lunacies with two new singles, ‘In Spite of It All’ and ‘Blossom’.

I designed the cover art for both releases on Spotify and Apple Music, working from a set of photos the artist took that inspired each song.
