About

I’m a digital and graphic designer with a background in retail ecommerce. At Cotton On Group I designed and produced campaigns across web, EDM and social, including licensed collaborations with FIFA, the NBA and Billie Eilish. I’ve also designed for artists, non-profits and local businesses. I strive to balance creative problem-solving and clarity when meeting the brief, all without losing what makes a brand feel like itself.
